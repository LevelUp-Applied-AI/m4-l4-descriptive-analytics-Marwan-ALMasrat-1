# Findings Report

## Dataset Overview
Dataset shape: (2000, 10)

## Key Insights
- GPA is slightly skewed.
- Study hours positively correlate with GPA.
- Internship students tend to have higher GPA.

## Hypothesis Results
- T-test: t=13.5644, p=0.0000
- ANOVA: F=0.6671, p=0.6148
- Correlation: r=0.6394, p=0.0000

## Recommendations
1. Encourage internships to improve academic performance.
2. Support students with low study hours.
3. Analyze department-level differences for targeted interventions.
